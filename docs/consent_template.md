# Participant Information and Informed Consent Form

**Project Title:** Real-Time Cognitive Load Estimation via Facial and Ocular Behavioral Signals (`cogload`)  
**Investigators:** Neeraj Gannamani, Rishabh Jain, Raunak Raj  
**Institution:** RV College of Engineering (RVCE), Department of Computer Science & Engineering  

---

### Purpose of the Study
You are invited to participate in a research study evaluating whether facial and eye behavior patterns (such as blink frequency, eye openness, gaze movement, and head pose) can serve as objective indicators of mental workload during computer tasks.

### What You Will Be Asked to Do
1. Provide basic demographic/setup information (pseudonymous ID, whether you wear glasses, room lighting condition).
2. Complete a short 90-second calibration routine (relaxing and looking at a screen target).
3. Perform short cognitive tasks (n-back memory tasks at varying difficulty levels) for approximately 3-minute blocks with rest intervals.
4. Answer a brief 6-question workload survey (NASA-TLX) after each block.
Total expected time: approximately 25-30 minutes.

### Privacy and Data Protection (Strict Privacy by Default)
- **NO RAW VIDEO OR PHOTOGRAPHS ARE SAVED TO DISK OR UPLOADED ANYWHERE.**
- The webcam stream is processed entirely in volatile memory on the local machine.
- Only derived numeric measurements (e.g. blink counts, gaze coordinates, head angles) are recorded.
- Data is stored strictly under an anonymous participant code (e.g., P001).
- You may request complete deletion of all data associated with your participant code at any time.

### Voluntary Participation
Participation is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any moment without penalty.
