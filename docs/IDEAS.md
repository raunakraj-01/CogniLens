# Scope Expansion and Stretch Ideas (Section 3.2 of PRD)

Items beyond the primary MVP to consider only after MVP phases pass:
- **S1. Second task family:** Mental arithmetic with click-to-answer for cross-task generalization.
- **S2. Sequence models:** 1D-CNN or lightweight LSTM on windowed feature time-series.
- **S3. Few-shot personalization:** Fine-tune or re-weight with participant-specific calibration windows.
- **S4. Facial tension features:** Brow furrow (corrugator supercilii proxy) and mouth tension (orbicularis oris proxy).
- **S5. Model explainability:** SHAP feature attribution summary panel.
